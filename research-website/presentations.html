Oral defense structure & guidelines

Standard presentation framework prescribed by the Faculty of Computing for undergraduate research panels.

01 Minutes 00–06

Problem Motivation & Research Gap

Justification of the national ICT education and transition crisis. Analysis of empirical benchmark gaps across existing algorithmic and rule-based guidance systems.

02 Minutes 06–16

Individual Architecture & Algorithmic Solutions

Each of the four group members presents their specific theoretical model: Hybrid Degree Recommendation (Member 1), Stacking Ensemble Career & Skill Gap AI (Member 2), Wearable Stress & Academic Wellbeing Analyser (Member 3), and Dual-Perspective Mentorship Matchmaking (Member 4).

03 Minutes 16–23

Live System Demonstration & Stress Testing

Real-time execution of the unified StudyFyX application showcasing microservices orchestration, Neo4j graph traversals, real-time inferencing, and responsive mobile interfaces.

04 Minutes 23–30

Q&A Examination & Viva Voce Defense

Rigorous defense of design decisions, mathematical formulations, ethical compliance, data security, and future post-graduate research opportunities before the panel of examiners.
