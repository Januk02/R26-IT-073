01 — Abstract

Project abstract & academic context

In Sri Lanka, students transitioning from secondary education (G.C.E. Advanced Level) to tertiary institutions, and subsequently into the competitive ICT workforce, face an uncoordinated and fragmented guidance environment. Traditional aptitude inventories operate in isolation from statutory University Grants Commission (UGC) Z-score admission thresholds and district quota constraints. In tertiary education, career advisement tools fail to quantitatively evaluate skill discrepancies against volatile industry job requirements, student psychological burnout frequently goes undetected prior to academic distress, and junior undergraduates lack structured access to verified professional mentors.

StudyFyX (R26-IT-073) investigates a holistic, multi-modal computational framework designed to mitigate these systemic gaps across four dedicated research dimensions: (1) an intelligent degree recommendation engine coupling rule-based backward inference with Random Forest classification; (2) a career pathway and skill gap analyzer utilizing TF-IDF weighted Multi-Label Stacking Ensemble learning and a Neo4j Knowledge Graph to calculate the mathematical Skill Gap Index ($SGI$); (3) a physiological stress detection subsystem applying digital Butterworth filtering and machine learning to wearable Photoplethysmography (PPG) and Heart Rate Variability (HRV) telemetry; and (4) an automated mentorship matchmaking system leveraging semantic Sentence-BERT profile embeddings and verified alumni onboarding pipelines.

Through comprehensive empirical evaluations, the proposed subsystems demonstrate high predictive accuracy and operational feasibility, presenting a unified computational architecture for student lifecycle guidance.

02 — Components

Four individual research components

Each component represents an autonomous research contribution with dedicated theoretical foundations, datasets, algorithms, and empirical validation.

Component 01 · Member 1

Intelligent Degree Recommender System

Formulates university admission pathway recommendations for G.C.E. A/L candidates based on stream criteria, Z-score percentiles, district quotas across Sri Lanka's 25 districts, and student interest vectors, combining backward-chaining rule engines with an 86.5% accuracy Random Forest classifier.

View component details
Component 02 · J A Wickremathilaka (IT22152114)

Career Pathway & Skill Gap AI Engine

Classifies multi-label skill profiles to predict the Top 3 industry career pathways using a Stacking Ensemble meta-learner (Random Forest, Linear SVC, Multinomial Naive Bayes → Logistic Regression), achieving 90.0% Top-3 accuracy. Mathematically quantifies readiness via the Skill Gap Index ($SGI$) and traverses Neo4j career pivot paths.

View component details
Component 03 · Member 3

Student Stress & Academic Wellbeing Analyser

Captures real-time physiological telemetry (HRV, PPG, skin temperature, and sleep metrics) from consumer smartwatches. Preprocesses noisy biosignals via digital Butterworth bandpass filters to classify multi-class cognitive stress states with 84.2% precision.

View component details
Component 04 · Member 4

Alumni Mentorship Matchmaking Platform

Engineers a bipartite matching framework using high-dimensional Sentence-BERT embeddings (87.5% semantic alignment) to pair undergraduates with verified industry alumni mentors, supported by automated CV attribute extraction and credential verification protocols.

View component details
